The Project

NZC-SGA2 T.16 – Cities in Greece is a 16-month assignment that supports the Greek National Platform for Climate Neutral Cities. It is part of NetZeroCities (SGA2 Task 1.6 – Sub-European Peer-to-Peer Collaboration), funded by Horizon Europe.

Project Overview

The project aims to strengthen the National-Local Dialogue between Greek municipalities and the central government, and to support the transition from diagnosis to implementation of climate neutrality actions.

It builds on the findings of previous NetZeroCities analytical work, including the review of Climate City Contracts (CCCs), stakeholder consultations, and the baseline report "Common Challenges & National Support Needs for Greek Mission Cities" (December 2025).

The project is structured around two National Ecosystem Events (2026 and 2027), supported by preparatory consultations, thematic dialogues, and ongoing ecosystem coordination throughout the 16-month period.

Objectives

The central objective of Task 1.6 is to strengthen an effective and functional National-Local Dialogue in Greece, improving cooperation among the Greek Mission Cities, national government, funding bodies, the market, the academic and research community, and other relevant actors within the climate neutrality ecosystem.

Bridge National & Local

Establish communication channels between municipalities and central government to address regulatory, financial, and technical barriers.

Knowledge Transfer

Exchange experiences and good practices among cities, focusing on "how" an action can be implemented.

From Planning to Implementation

Translate strategic priorities from CCCs into specific, implementable, and financeable interventions.

Activate the Ecosystem

Engage actors beyond Local Government: central government, financial institutions, businesses, academia, and civil society.

Strengthen Mission Cities

Support the six Greek Mission Cities in presenting real needs, exchanging experiences, and identifying joint actions.

Establish a Replicable Process

Create a systematic national-local dialogue process that can be replicated and sustained beyond the project.

Thematic Focus

The main thematic focus of Task 1.6 is the decarbonisation of the built environment and the improvement of building energy efficiency. This reflects the significant contribution of the building stock to energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions, and the need to move from planning to practical implementation.

Indicative critical areas include:

  • Utilisation and effectiveness of funding programmes such as Exoikonomo
  • Access to and utilisation of energy data, including data from HEDNO
  • Development and utilisation of ESCO models
  • Use of Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs)
  • Development of Climate Investment Plans (CIPs)

Intervention Logic

CCC Actions → 19 Common Areas of Action → Common Implementation Needs → National-Local Dialogue → CID / BFD → Specific, Implementable & Financeable Actions

Governance Structure

The implementation of the assignment is based on a multi-level governance structure, with clearly distinguished roles and responsibilities.

Climate-KIC

Contracting Authority

Responsible for the institutional, administrative, and financial framework of the contract. Ensures alignment with the broader objectives of NetZeroCities.

Resilient Cities Network (R-Cities)

Country Coordinator for Greece

Provides strategic coordination at national level, contributes to the design of national events, and reviews key deliverables.

SMR Consultants Ltd.

Local Contractor

Responsible for operational implementation, stakeholder engagement, preparatory meetings, and the organisation of the two National Ecosystem Events.

Timeline & Milestones

The project runs from July 2026 to October 2027, organised into two interconnected implementation cycles.

July – August 2026

Kick-off & Operational Setup

Finalisation of working methodology, coordination mechanisms, initial stakeholder mapping, and planning for the first cycle.

September 2026

Deliverable 1.1 – Workplan 2026 & Baseline Report

Detailed operational plan for 2026 and initial diagnostic mapping of the national-local ecosystem.

September – October 2026

Stakeholder Engagement & Preparatory Consultations

Online/hybrid meetings, bilateral consultations, and targeted communication with key stakeholders.

5–6 November 2026

1st National Ecosystem Event – Kalamata

Two-day event alongside the KEDE annual conference. Includes thematic sessions, interactive workshops, CID, and BFD.

December 2026

Deliverable 2.1 – Final Report & Post-Event Outputs

Assessment of the first cycle, KPI documentation, bilingual Policy Recommendation Note, and communication material.

March 2027

Deliverable 1.2 – Workplan 2027 & Baseline Update

Updated workplan and baseline, building on the outcomes of the first cycle.

March – September 2027

Intermediate Engagement & Event 2 Preparation

Continued ecosystem activation, thematic consultations, and preparation for the second event.

September – October 2027

2nd National Ecosystem Event

Assessment of progress since the first event, deepening dialogue, and identifying next steps.

October 2027

Project Completion & Final Deliverables

Deliverable 2.2 – Final Report & Integrated Project Synthesis, and Deliverable 3 – NZC CESF Final Delivery Report.
